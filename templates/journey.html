What Mira will and will not tell you

Mira lists properties it can point at a source for: the modes, the number of transfers, how far you walk at the end, which entrance you arrive at, and the hours women's transport runs. Where it has a fare it can characterise, it shows the figure and says how confident it is; where it cannot, it shows no figure at all rather than a number it cannot stand behind.

It does not give a route a safety rating — it has no way to verify one, and it does not know your neighbourhood, the time of day, or what you are comfortable with.
