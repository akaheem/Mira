Mira does not require an account, and does not keep what you type. Your description is used once to choose a process, then discarded. It is never sent to an AI service, and Mira writes no copy of it to any database, file or log. It is sent by POST rather than placed in the web address, so it does not land in your browser history either.

One honest limit: Mira runs on a hosting platform that keeps its own standard request logs, which this project does not control. So we do not claim nothing is recorded anywhere — only that Mira itself stores nothing. Exactly what happens, in detail.
