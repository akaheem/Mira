Why there is no safety score here

A number would be easier to read than this list, and that is exactly the problem. Mira cannot verify that a route is safe, and a score would hide that. What it can do is give you the properties it has a source for — the fare, the hour of the last departure, whether women's transport is running, how far you walk at the end — and let you decide, because you know the context and Mira does not.

Mira has no timetable. It can tell you when the last recorded departure is, but not which departure to take or when the next one leaves. So it does not compute a “leave by” time: that would need a schedule it does not have, and being wrong about it could leave you stranded. Check the times above against your own journey.

Sending this to someone

Sharing builds a short summary of a route above — modes, duration, fare, last departure, final walk — inside your own browser, and hands it to your phone's normal share sheet. You choose who receives it.

Mira is not involved in that: the summary is not sent to our servers, and we keep no record of it or of who you sent it to. Mira also never asks for your live location. There is no tracking link, because a link that reports back would defeat the point.
